# Activate inactive accounts for modelling

This is an explicit, optional stage **after disaggregation and before final
exports or modelling**. It does not change the MARIO/CVXLab split formulation or
rerun an optimisation solver. It creates a separate MARIO scenario, preserving
the accepted source checkpoint.

```bash
PYTHONPATH=src python scripts/finalize_saved_database.py \
  --database /path/to/accepted/database \
  --baseline /path/to/original/unsplit/baseline \
  --output /path/to/new/model-ready/database \
  --policy config/zero-activity-finalization.json
```

Use the compatible MARIO checkout/environment documented for the disaggregation.
The stage calls existing MARIO APIs; it requires no new MARIO or CVXLab changes.
The reference baseline must have the same regions. Monetary units must be M USD.
Output must be a new directory. E remains pending.

## Frozen policy

The versioned policy file specifies 10 USD of additional domestic household
final demand per account whose original X is at most 1 USD. The 1 USD threshold
also excludes numerical dust from global recipe donors. The demand epsilon and
threshold are expressed in USD and converted to the database's M USD units.
They are distinct from the entropy denominator epsilon.

Demand is **added**, never substituted for existing Y. Original signed final
demand categories are retained. Regional recipes for productive accounts remain
unchanged. Their output and monetary flows can change through the IO system.

## Recipes and regional sourcing

For sector s, the global intermediate coefficient for input i is the sum of
input i purchases across productive destination accounts, divided by the sum of
those destinations' output. Supply origins are summed before averaging.
Value-added coefficients use the same output weights. Capital and Labor must
remain nonnegative; Tax and TTM retain their signs. Global recipes are not
arbitrarily rescaled to force exact cost closure when the source has inherited
accounting residuals.

If a sector has no productive observed account, an explicit recipe is required.
For the current NMC and SDH cases, the stage reads their checksum-verified exact
accepted input workbooks, including links to subsequently inserted suppliers.
Inventory reference recipes are averaged using original baseline parent output.
No parent recipe is silently substituted. Missing or unsupported inventory
information is an error, rather than an invented recipe.

Each global input coefficient is then distributed across origins, using the
first available pattern in this fixed order:

1. Purchases of that input by productive members of the buyer's parent family
   in the destination region.
2. That input's destination market: productive intermediate buyers and positive
   final-demand purchases.
3. Original baseline input-parent purchases by the buyer's parent in that region.
4. The original baseline input-parent destination market.
5. Global producer output shares.

This follows the add-sectors engine's use of parent/region purchase flows for
regionalizing a GLOBAL inventory input. Existing supplier regions with output
above the threshold are used where available. For globally inactive suppliers,
baseline parent producer locations provide the explicit reference. No arbitrary
all-domestic or equal-across-countries allocation is used.

`regional-sourcing.parquet` records the chosen rule, input coefficient and every
origin share. `activated-accounts.parquet` records each original X and recipe
source. Both are generated by the stage, not manually maintained.

## MARIO execution

The stage uses `clone_scenario` and `update_scenarios` to install z, v and Y,
then `reset_to_coefficients`. `calc_all`, with
`ComputeOptions(backend_override="sparse_direct")`, solves for X directly and
rebuilds Z and V. It does not construct the full Leontief inverse.
`reset_to_flows` clears stale coefficient representations; X, z and v are then
reconstructed and exported through `to_parquet`.

The source E file is carried byte-for-byte, with its pending status. Unavailable
e coefficients are omitted. Flow and coefficient source matrices are never
edited in the accepted checkpoint. The finalized state has a distinct format;
it must not be passed off as an original resumable sector-step checkpoint.

## Reports and interpretation

The report records production, value added, final demand, accounting residuals
and foreign bilateral trade changes. `production-changes.csv` includes all
accounts, including productive accounts whose quantity changes indirectly.

The extra final demand and the replacement of inconsistent inactive-column
inputs change quantities. Original output/trade constraints therefore become
**historical disaggregation references**, not constraints of this final stage.
A finalized dashboard labels them accordingly and identifies assigned recipes
in cost-structure hovers. The original dashboard and constraints remain intact.

Finite nonnegative production and coefficients are checked before publication.
Column accounting residuals inherited through existing recipes are reported;
this stage does not introduce a separate balancing optimisation. Economic
validation and environmental recalculation remain separate tasks.

## First 61-region application, 23 November 2026

The current 145-sector result activated 1,984 accounts: 1,913 exact production
zeros and 71 positive outputs at most 1 USD. Total added demand was 19,840 USD.
The direct solve and checks completed without changing solver/model parameters.

Gross output decreased by about 736.27 million USD as spurious purchases in
inactive columns were replaced. Total value added increased by about 99,724 USD;
the largest foreign bilateral change was about 5.45 million USD. The maximum
column-accounting residual remained about 246,320 USD, essentially its original
level. These measured effects are documented, not assumed to equal the epsilon
injection alone. The original 69-sector insertion history is unchanged.
